Expansion to Germany

Successful business in Russia — and expansion to Germany? Often it's not the product that blocks you, but restrictions, compliance, and no local partners. We deliver in Germany: market analysis, sales setup, company formation, contracts, registrations — as an individual project. You provide capital and strategy, Beraterium coordinates on-site execution.

Many entrepreneurs with running businesses in Russia want the German market — but sanctions, regulatory hurdles, and missing local contacts hold them back.
