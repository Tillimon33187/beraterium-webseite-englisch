FIT CHECK

Is a risk analysis right for your self-employment?

A structured risk analysis is worth it if at least one of these applies:

  • Your revenue depends entirely on you as a person — if you stop, revenue stops
  • You employ 1–5 people or work with a fixed network of collaborators
  • One main client accounts for a large share of your revenue
  • You have no clear picture of what an outage would actually cost you
  • From €97for the compact risk check (30 min)
  • Top 5–10risks assessed in euros and prioritised
  • From €1,725for full risk consulting with report
  • 2× money backrelevance and value guarantee

SCOPE (RA-02)

What do you get with risk consulting (RA-02)?

One workshop (2–3 hours), facilitated by us. Goal: turn gut feeling into a prioritised list assessed in euros — without jumping straight into implementation planning.

  • Each risk assessed by damage in euros and likelihood
  • Top 5–10 risks named and prioritised — basis for insurance or bank talks
  • Outcome documented as a report, including follow-up call to interpret results
  • Ideal if you want clarity on your risk picture first, without immediate measure planning

THREE RISKS WITH NO BACKUP

What happens if these three risks stay hidden?

As a solo professional you carry every risk alone — no works council, no cover, no IT department.

  • Incapacity

    There is no employer sick pay — 4–6 weeks off or burnout can threaten your livelihood while fixed costs continue. More: Protect yourself as self-employed.

  • Client concentration

    If one main client drives most of your revenue, their budget cycle decides your survival. More: Risks for self-employed professionals.

  • No cover

    Without colleagues or a network contact with access to your projects, everything stops when you do — including towards clients. More: Identify key-person risk.
